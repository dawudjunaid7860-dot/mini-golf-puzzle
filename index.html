How to Play

Level 1 is simple: three stars, one spinner, one hole — seen from straight above.

➤
Drag back, aim, let goPull back on the course. The straight arrow and dotted line show exactly where the ball will go.
▬
Watch the power barThe bar at the bottom fills green → yellow → red as you pull further. More fill = harder shot.
★
Grab the stars in order: 1 → 2 → 3The glowing, pulsing star is next. The others wait their turn.
✦
Dodge the spinnerOne spinning arm in the middle of the course. Time your shot past it — if it clips you, you'll get knocked off line!
◎
Then sink itThe hole stays locked until all 3 stars are yours. Then it lights up green — roll it in to win!
